Loan terms calculator

The current demo schedule uses a flat 30% interest charge, with 10-day repayment for amounts up to and including KSh 3,000 and 15-day repayment for amounts above KSh 3,000.

Before real deployment, check applicable Kenyan lending, licensing, privacy and consumer-protection requirements. Display the full cost, repayment dates, late-payment policy and complaint contact before customers accept a loan. Do not use this prototype to store real passwords, identity documents or financial records.
